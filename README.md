# DoMore – AI Notebook

This is the official documentation repository for DoMore. Learn how to organize screenshots, voice memos, and documents into topic notes and ask questions across your notes.

DoMore is an AI notebook that organizes screenshots, voice memos, documents, links, and quick thoughts by topic. You can save everything together. DoMore brings related details from different sources into focused notes and keeps the original context. After you close the app, its background agent continues looking for connections between new material and earlier notes, including decisions to revisit and tasks to follow up on.

You can ask questions about your own material or turn it into flashcards, interactive web pages, and podcasts to study or share.

## How does one batch become several notes?

Imagine you leave a product meeting with a recording, two whiteboard photos, and a PDF of customer feedback. The team discussed both the sign-up flow and the next release date. Several comments in the PDF relate to sign-up.

Save the materials together, and DoMore can group the sign-up discussion with the relevant customer comments while organizing the release discussion into a separate note. You can review, edit, move, or delete the notes it creates.

If you later add a customer report about sign-up, the background agent can connect it to the earlier discussion and bring the team's decision back to your attention.

## What can I capture?

| Material | What you can do with it |
| --- | --- |
| Quick thoughts and web links | Save an idea or reference while it is fresh and keep it with related material. |
| Photos, screenshots, and scans | Extract printed or handwritten text with optical character recognition (OCR). Turn a whiteboard photo or paper note into editable text. |
| Voice memos, meeting recordings, and video | Record directly or import audio or video for transcription. Pull summaries, decisions, and action items from the transcript. |
| PDFs, Word documents, Excel workbooks, and PowerPoint slides | Bring papers, reports, spreadsheets, and lecture slides into your notebook for search, questions, translation, or summaries. |
| Tables and formulas | Extract editable tables from images or PDFs. Convert photographed math formulas into LaTeX. |

## How do I find answers in my notes?

Ask across your knowledge base, or attach a particular document to focus the conversation on that source. For example:

- “What changes to the sign-up flow have we discussed, and which ones did we agree on?”
- “What is the main claim in this paper, and what evidence do the authors give?”
- “Which follow-up tasks came up in this meeting, and which ones have an owner?”

If you remember a person, project, topic, or approximate date, you can also browse your notes that way.

## What can I create or export?

| Goal | Output options |
| --- | --- |

| Build study materials | Flashcards, quizzes, or slides based on your sources |

## Where does DoMore fit into my day?

- **Study and research.** Keep lecture recordings, board photos, and papers together. Extract a formula from a photo, ask about the evidence in a paper, or turn your notes into a quiz.
- **Meetings and projects.** Organize recordings, whiteboards, and project documents by issue. When a topic comes up again, find earlier decisions and outstanding tasks.
- **Design and writing.** Collect reference images, links, and spoken ideas. When a new brief arrives, find relevant examples and draw on your notes for a first draft.
- **Travel and personal notes.** Keep bookings, itineraries, photos, and voice memos together. Look up a reservation before departure and add observations along the way.

## Accounts, sync, and privacy

**Do I need an account?** You can use the core features without creating an account or providing an email address. You choose what to save and whether to enable auto-save or sync. With sync enabled, your knowledge base is accessible on mobile and on the web.

**How is my material processed when I use AI?** DoMore uses Google Cloud Vertex AI to process the inputs needed for features such as text recognition, summaries, and document Q&A. Under Vertex AI's data governance terms, the prompts and content you submit are not used to train Google's foundation models.

**Can I export or delete my data?** You can export notes, delete saved content in the app, or delete your account. Confirming account deletion revokes access immediately and starts a 30-day recovery period before deletion becomes final. See the [trust and privacy overview](https://app.domore.bot/trust) and [privacy policy](https://app.domore.bot/privacy) for details.
