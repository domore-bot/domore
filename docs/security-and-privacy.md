# DoMore security and privacy

## When does DoMore process or save my material?

Using a cloud feature can send the material needed for that request for processing. For example, scanning a page may process the image or its text on your device or in the cloud. Saving a result, including through auto-save or sync, can store it in your personal knowledge base. Core features are available without registration.

## Who processes AI requests? Is my content used for training?

DoMore identifies Google Cloud Vertex AI as its AI provider for features such as text recognition, translation, summaries, and chat. Under Vertex AI’s data governance terms, Google does not use the prompts and content submitted through DoMore to train its foundation models. Processing can take place outside your country, including in the United States.

## How is saved content protected?

DoMore encrypts data in transit and at rest and limits access according to the permissions needed. Google Cloud and Firebase services support storage and sync. Saved content is associated with an installation, device, or account identifier, as applicable.

## What does usage analytics collect?

DoMore measures usage such as screen views in the mobile app and page views in the web app. Note text, files, and messages are excluded from analytics. On the product website at app.domore.bot, analytics runs only after you accept the analytics notice. DoMore does not sell personal information or use your content for targeted advertising.

## How can I remove my data?

You can delete individual saved items in the app. If you confirm deletion of your entire account, active access ends immediately. You have 30 days to restore the account before deletion becomes final.

For full details, read the [DoMore Privacy Policy](https://app.domore.bot/privacy) and [Trust & Privacy overview](https://app.domore.bot/trust). For questions about your data, contact [support@domore.bot](mailto:support@domore.bot).
