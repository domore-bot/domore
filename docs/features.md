# DoMore features

DoMore is an AI notebook for material that arrives in pieces: a thought you record on your phone, a whiteboard photo, a link, or a document. Save them together and DoMore organizes them by subject. You can return to those notes to find a decision, edit a formula, reuse a table, or build something to share.

## Turn one meeting into several topic notes

Imagine a meeting covers a product launch and a hiring decision. The recording covers both, but the whiteboard photo only concerns the launch. DoMore can separate the two subjects and group the photo with the relevant discussion. Each note retains its source context. You can review the results and edit, move, or delete the notes.

## DoMore keeps working after you close the app

You can save a thought, a voice memo, a screenshot, or a document whenever it comes up. Each time you add something, the background agent has another piece of information to compare with what you’ve already saved.

Even after you close the app, the agent keeps looking for new connections across your materials. It filters out repeated information and brings you conclusions worth considering, opportunities you might want to explore, and items worth following up on.

## Capture and extract information

| What you have | What you can do with it |
| --- | --- |
| A voice memo, meeting recording, or video | Record directly or import a file for transcription. Use the transcript to draw up a summary or action list. |
| A whiteboard photo or handwritten page | Use optical character recognition (OCR) to recover editable text. Convert a photographed math formula to LaTeX. |
| A table in a scan or PDF | Use Table Parser to extract the data and export it as CSV or XLSX for use in a spreadsheet. |
| A PDF, Word document, Excel workbook, or PowerPoint deck | Import it into your notebook. Translate a passage, summarize a report, or ask about a specific detail. |
| A screenshot, web link, or quick thought | Save it alongside related material. Browse the collection later by topic, person, project, or date. |

## Ask a question across notes or focus on one source

When a decision spans several meetings, ask your knowledge base a question such as “What did we decide about the launch date?” When the wording of one document matters, attach that document and narrow the question: “Which dates does this brief give for the pilot?” DoMore uses your saved material as context. With sync enabled, you can access your knowledge base on mobile and on the web.

## Turn your notes into something you can use

- **Study a lecture.** Combine lecture audio and board photos, organize the concepts into an outline or mind map, and generate flashcards or a quiz for review.
- **Prepare a creative brief.** Collect references, client documents, and spoken ideas. Pull out the requirements, find relevant material in earlier work, and use it as context for a draft.
- **Share a project update.** Turn meeting notes into a summary with action items. Create an interactive slide page for readers or a Studio podcast for listeners.
- **Continue editing elsewhere.** Export a written note as Markdown, DOCX, PDF, HTML, or plain text. Use CSV or XLSX when you need the values from a table.
