# DoMore FAQ

## What happens if I save several topics at once?

DoMore groups related material into topic notes. For example, a recording that covers two projects can contribute to separate notes. You can edit, move, or delete the notes it creates. Its background agent continues looking for connections as your collection grows.

## Which files can I import?

DoMore accepts PDF, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files, as well as photos and screenshots. You can save a web link, record a voice memo, or import audio or video for transcription.

## Can I scan handwritten notes or math formulas?

Yes. DoMore can turn handwriting and printed text into editable text. For math, it can convert a photographed formula into LaTeX so you can edit the notation.

## Can I get a spreadsheet from a table in a PDF or image?

Yes. DoMore can extract the rows and columns and export the table as CSV or XLSX for use in Excel.

## Can I ask about one document or search across all my notes?

Both. Attach a document to focus a conversation on that source, or ask a question across your knowledge base. You can also browse by topic, person, project, or date.

## What can I export or share?

Export written notes as Markdown, PDF, DOCX, HTML, or plain text. To present or study your material, you can create a podcast or share an interactive page with slides, flashcards, or a quiz.

## Can I access my notes on mobile and on the web?

Yes. Enable sync to access your saved knowledge base from the mobile and web apps.

## Do I need an account? What happens to the files I submit?

You can use DoMore’s core features without registering. Cloud features may send the relevant input for processing. See [Security and privacy](security-and-privacy.md) for details on processing, storage, and deletion.
